Your data

Export

Everything in your journal (ties, plans, people, locations and tags) in one file. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Import

Add to your journal from a Rope Journal export, or from a spreadsheet of ties saved as CSV (download a template). People, locations and plans are matched to yours by name, and new ones are added. Ties already in your journal are skipped. You'll see what will happen before anything is saved.
